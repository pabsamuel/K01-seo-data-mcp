Privacy and data flow.

Updated 22 October 2026. Publisher: Atesen Software.

Local MCP server

Your Google/Bing credentials and license stay on your computer. The server reads credential files only to authenticate requests to Google. Bing and Google API keys go directly to their respective APIs. IndexNow receives your submitted URLs and its verification key. We receive no SEO reports, search queries or provider credentials.

Network destinations are restricted to oauth2.googleapis.com, www.googleapis.com, searchconsole.googleapis.com, ssl.bing.com, api.indexnow.org and chromeuxreport.googleapis.com. Redirects are disabled. Installing via npm or a directory involves those services separately. Your MCP host and AI provider may process returned reports according to their policies.

The local usage file stores date, count and last-call timestamp. There is no telemetry or background license check. Delete the local state folder and private credential/license files to remove your local records.

Website and optional billing

Cloudflare serves this site and may process request metadata under its service terms. No custom analytics or tracking cookies are used. Billing is currently disabled. When activated, Paddle processes payment and customer data; our licensing database stores a random installation token, signed license (plan, subscription ID, issuance and expiry) and webhook event IDs. It does not store SEO data, email addresses or payment-card data. Checkout stores your random installation token in your browser so you can retrieve your key.

Billing records are retained while the license is active and for up to 24 months for support and reconciliation; deletion is handled through support. Statutory accounting records held by Paddle may have different retention. Offline keys cannot be revoked immediately after refunds and expire at the paid-period end. Avoid sharing your installation token.

For access/deletion requests use publisher support. Never send credentials or reports with personal data.
