# SEO Data MCP
Short description: Bring Search Console, Bing, IndexNow, PageSpeed and CrUX into your MCP client using your own credentials.

SEO Data MCP runs locally over stdio. Query search performance, list sitemaps, inspect indexed URLs, review Bing crawl issues, submit your own URLs to IndexNow and summarize page experience. Google/Bing credentials remain on your device. SEO report data does not pass through Atesen Software. Your MCP host receives the tool results.

FREE AND PAID LICENSES: Free includes Search Console queries, sitemap lists and PageSpeed, limited to 20 attempts per UTC day. Starter is planned at $12/month (all eight tools, 1,000 attempts/day). Pro is planned at $29/month (all eight tools, 5,000 attempts/day). Paid checkout is not open during this preview. Paid licenses validate offline and expire at the paid-period end. Provider quotas and access requirements still apply. No indexing or ranking guarantee.

Requires Node.js 22+ and your own provider credentials. No paid LLM API, proxy or remote SEO hosting is required. IndexNow performs a write operation and needs explicit confirmation and domain verification.
